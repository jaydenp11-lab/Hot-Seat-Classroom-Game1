How To Play in the Classroom

Hot Seat Rules

Place a chair in front of the classroom facing away from the projector. One student sits in the Hot Seat. The rest of the class shouts out descriptive clues (without saying the secret word) before the timer runs out!

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts:
  • Right Arrow (→) or C - Mark Correct (+100)
  • Left Arrow (←) or X or Space - Pass
  • P - Pause / Resume Timer
  • M - Mute Audio
